How to Play

Take turns placing X and O marks on the 3x3 grid. The first player to line up three marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins. If all nine cells fill with no winner, it's a draw.

Game Modes

2 Player - take turns on the same device.

Vs CPU - play against the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty. Hard mode plays perfectly and cannot be beaten, only tied.

Pause Menu

Press 0 or the Left Softkey during a game to pause. From there you can resume, restart, open Settings/Help/About, or return to the Main Menu.
